New to crypto? Read this first

Is crypto legal in Malaysia?

Yes. Buying and selling crypto is legal. Use an exchange registered with the Securities Commission Malaysia (check the SC's list of Recognized Market Operators). Unregistered platforms can be shut down and you may lose your money.

How much should I put in?

Only money you can afford to lose completely. Crypto can fall 50% or more. Never use savings for rent, food or emergencies.

What is a stablecoin?

A coin built to stay at about US$1, like USDT or USDC. In Ringgit its price only moves when the RM/USD rate moves.

How do I spot a scam?

Guaranteed returns, "send crypto to double it", strangers on Telegram or WhatsApp offering to manage your money, and pressure to act fast are all red flags.
